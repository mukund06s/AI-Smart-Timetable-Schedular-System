## 3. WHAT TO PRESENT (Presentation Content)

### A. Project Definition & Background
- **Problem Statement:** Clearly define the problem you're addressing
- **Purpose:** Why this project is needed
- **Need of the project:** Justification for your work
- **Novelty:** What makes your idea unique or different from existing work
- **Objectives:** Clearly defined and measurable goals

### B. Scope of the Project
- What your project will cover
- What it will NOT cover (limitations)
- Target users/audience

### C. Tools & Technology
- **Hardware requirements** (if applicable)
- **Software/Languages/Frameworks** to be used
- **Development environment**
- **Libraries/Dependencies**

### D. Feasibility Analysis
- **Technical feasibility:** Is it technically possible?
- **Resource feasibility:** Do you have access to required resources?
- **Time feasibility:** Can it be completed within the semester?
- **Constraints considered:** Budget, time, skills, infrastructure
- **Risk assessment:** Potential challenges and mitigation plans

### E. Project Schedule (Gantt Chart)
A detailed timeline for the entire semester covering:
- Topic finalization
- Literature review completion
- Design phase
- Development phase
- Testing phase
- Documentation
- Final presentation preparation

## 4. EVALUATION CRITERIA (10 Marks Total)

### Parameter A: Identification of Problem Statement, Purpose & Need (5 Marks)

| Level | Expectations |
|-------|--------------|
| **Far Exceeds (5)** | Detailed and extensive explanation of project idea, purpose, need, novelty, and clearly defined objectives |
| **Exceeds (4)** | Good explanation of project idea, purpose, and need |
| **Meets (3)** | Average explanation of project idea, purpose, and need |
| **Below (2)** | No great explanation |

### Parameter B: Feasibility of Implementation (5 Marks)

| Level | Expectations |
|-------|--------------|
| **Far Exceeds (5)** | Feasibility done considering all constraints and well explained |
| **Exceeds (4)** | Has idea about constraints and considered feasibility |
| **Meets (3)** | Feasibility yet to be considered; Has knowledge about constraints |
| **Below (2)** | Not considered constraints and feasibility |

## 7. PREPARATION CHECKLIST

### Before the Presentation
- [ ] Finalize project topic with mentor
- [ ] Complete literature review
- [ ] Prepare PowerPoint presentation (20-25 slides recommended)
- [ ] Get presentation cross-checked by guide
- [ ] Prepare Gantt Chart
- [ ] Prepare Topic Approval Form
- [ ] Practice presentation (5-8 minutes typical)
- [ ] Prepare for Q&A session
- [ ] Arrange formal attire
- [ ] Confirm your panel and schedule

### Presentation Structure (Suggested)
1. **Title Slide** (Project title, team members, guide name)
2. **Agenda/Outline**
3. **Introduction** (Background, context)
4. **Problem Statement**
5. **Objectives**
6. **Literature Review Summary** (Key findings, research gaps)
7. **Proposed Solution Overview**
8. **Scope of Work**
9. **Tools & Technology**
10. **Feasibility Analysis**
11. **Gantt Chart** (Project Timeline)
12. **Expected Outcomes**
13. **References**
14. **Conclusion**

---

## 8. AFTER THE REVIEW

### Post-Review Actions
- Note feedback/comments from panel
- Get your **Topic Approval Form** signed by:
  - Project Guide
  - Panel Members
  - HoD (as required)
- Make any changes in project title if suggested
- Address any areas of concern identified

---

## 9. COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

- ❌ Vague or unclear problem statement
- ❌ No proper literature review
- ❌ Unrealistic timeline or scope
- ❌ Not considering constraints
- ❌ Poor presentation design (cluttered, too much text)
- ❌ Not knowing your own project details
- ❌ Late submission to guide
- ❌ Informal attire
- ❌ Team member absence
- ❌ No demonstration of novelty
